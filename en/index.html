04 FAQ

Everything you want to know

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on project complexity, number of sections and features. Contact us for a free estimate — every project is unique and we offer solutions tailored to your budget.

How long does development take?

We deliver a standard site in 7–14 days from design approval. More complex projects may take up to 4 weeks. Speed partly depends on your availability for feedback and materials.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes — we offer monthly maintenance and updates for €20/month. It includes regular content updates, performance monitoring and technical support. No surprises, no hidden costs.

What does Lighthouse 100/100 mean for me?

Lighthouse 100/100 means your site is as fast, accessible and SEO-optimized as possible by Google's standards. The result: better search rankings, lower bounce rate and more conversions.

Do you work outside Serbia?

Absolutely. The whole process is remote — video consultations, online deliverables, payment by bank transfer or PayPal. We work with clients across the region.
